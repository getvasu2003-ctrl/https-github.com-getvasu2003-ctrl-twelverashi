12Rashi Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Deletion Policy and global privacy regulations, users of 12Rashi have the right to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

1. Data That Will Be Deleted:

  • Astrological Profile: Full name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth.
  • Saved Charts: All generated Janam Kundli charts, Gun Milan records, and family vault charts.
  • Consultation Transcripts: Past chat consultation histories and questions submitted to astrologers.
  • Contact Info: Registered phone number, email address, and notification tokens.

2. Data Retention Policy:

Past billing invoice records (payment gateway transaction IDs) may be retained for accounting and tax compliance as required by Indian financial regulations for up to 180 days before automated purging. No credit/debit card or banking data is stored.

✓ Your deletion request has been logged. Our Data Protection Team will verify and purge all associated records within 48 to 72 business hours. A confirmation notification will be sent.

Alternative Method: Inside the App

If you still have the 12Rashi app installed, you can wipe your data immediately with 1 tap:

  1. Open the 12Rashi app.
  2. Scroll down to the footer and tap Legal & Compliance (or open your Profile).
  3. Select "Delete My Account & Birth Data".
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your local and cloud birth data will be immediately erased.
