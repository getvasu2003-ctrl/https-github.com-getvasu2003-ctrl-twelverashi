12Rashi Privacy Policy

Welcome to 12Rashi ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide Vedic astrology consultations, Janam Kundli analytics, horoscopes, and spiritual services. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act and global data protection standards.

1. Information We Collect

  • Astrological Profile Data: Full Name, Date of Birth, Exact Time of Birth, Place of Birth, and Gender. This information is strictly required to calculate astronomical planetary charts (Janam Kundli, Dasha, Gochar, and Navamsha).
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number and email address for account authentication via OTP and consultation order receipts.
  • Consultation Records: Chat messages and consultation transcripts conducted with verified astrologers to maintain your consultation history.
  • Transaction Data: Payment order IDs processed via PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (e.g., Cashfree). We never store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate astronomical birth charts, Kundli Milan matching, and daily horoscopes.
  • To connect you with certified astrologers for live chat, audio, and video consultations.
  • To send transaction receipts, appointment alerts, and service updates.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with applicable laws.

3. Data Protection & Encryption

All communication between your device and our servers is secured using Industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL encryption). Sensitive astrological and profile records are securely stored on Google Cloud Firebase with strict role-based access controls.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or birth details with any advertising brokers. Data is only shared with:

  • Verified astrologers solely for the duration of your active consultation.
  • Certified payment gateways for completing wallet recharges.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (Google Cloud / Firebase) for hosting and security.

5. User Data Deletion Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You can request deletion of your account and all associated Kundli records at any time directly through the app settings or by emailing our Data Protection Officer at: support@12rashi.com.
